Restoring purchases

Your purchases belong to the store account you bought them with, not to a Bubba, Inc. account. If you reinstall an app, factory reset, or switch to a new device, sign in with the same store account and restore.

iPhone and iPad

Open the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account that made the original purchase — a different Apple Account cannot see those purchases.

Android, Amazon, and Meta Horizon

Purchases restore automatically when you open the app while signed in to the same Google, Amazon, or Meta account. If they do not appear, force-close the app and reopen it while connected to the internet.

Windows and Xbox

Sign in to the Microsoft Store with the account that made the purchase, then reopen the app. If the app still shows items as locked, open the Microsoft Store, check for pending updates, and try again.

The purchase went through but nothing unlocked

This is almost always a connection or sign-in problem rather than a lost purchase. Work through these in order:

  1. Confirm you are connected to the internet. Purchases are verified against the store, which needs a live connection.
  2. Confirm you are signed in with the same store account that made the purchase.
  3. Force-close the app completely and reopen it.
  4. Use the restore steps above.
  5. Restart the device, then reopen the app.

If none of that works, contact us using the form at the top of this page with the app name, your platform, the item you bought, and the date. Do not send a screenshot of your receipt with your card details visible — we do not need them and cannot use them.

Content won't play or load

Media in our apps is either bundled with the app or streamed. If something you own will not open:

  • Check your connection. Streamed items need a stable one; switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data mid-playback can interrupt it.
  • Check free storage. A device that is nearly full cannot cache media reliably.
  • Update the app. Older versions can point at content that has since moved.
  • Turn the volume up and check silent mode. Some previews are silent by design, but the full item is not.

Still stuck? Send us the item name and a short description of what you see instead.

cVision on Discord

The Activity is blank or stuck loading

Close the Activity, wait about a minute, and launch it again. Our service goes to sleep when nobody is using it, and the first launch after a quiet period can take up to a minute to wake up. If it is still blank after a second try, restart the Discord app entirely — a browser refresh is not enough in the desktop client.

I unlocked a file and it played, but now it wants payment again

That is expected. Each unlock in cVision is a single-use purchase for one randomly selected file. Owning one unlock does not grant the whole library.

I don't want cVision to have access to my Discord account anymore

Open User Settings → Authorized Apps in Discord and remove cVision. This does not remove purchases you have already made.

Which Discord permissions does it use?

Only enough to confirm who you are and what you have purchased. The full detail is in our privacy policy.

Game problems

Controller isn't working

Connect the controller before launching the game, then restart the game if it was already open. Our games accept keyboard, touch, and gamepad input at the same time, so you do not need to switch modes.

The game crashes or won't start

Restart the device, make sure the app is fully updated, and confirm you have free storage. If it still fails, tell us your exact device model and OS version — that is usually enough for us to reproduce it.

Multiplayer won't connect

Both players need a working internet connection, and some networks block the ports games use. Try a different network, such as a phone hotspot, to narrow it down.

I lost my progress

Game progress is saved on your device. Uninstalling the game, clearing its data, or resetting the device removes that save, and we cannot recover it because we never had a copy.

  • Device and operating system version
  • What you did, step by step
  • What you expected, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you can capture one
